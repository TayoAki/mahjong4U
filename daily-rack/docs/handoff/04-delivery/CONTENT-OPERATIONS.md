# Content operations

## Content unit
A daily puzzle contains a stable ID, publication calendar day, immutable revision, mode, deal of thirteen tile instances, section choices, primary question, reviewed suggestions, per-tile reasons, acceptable alternatives, review metadata and clip script. Only approved content is published.

## Workflow
1. Author an original practice deal and question within the approved content approach.
2. Validate exactly thirteen unique instance IDs, permitted face codes/multiplicities and pass restrictions for the chosen ruleset.
3. Evaluate the proposed direction and pass with a documented method. Simulations must record the assumptions, candidate set and ruleset.
4. Have a qualified teacher review the suggestion and language. Where there is disagreement, include an alternative or explain the tradeoff.
5. Review card references, artwork and phrasing under the approach chosen by the owner; do not reproduce the annual card.
6. Preview the native screen and export timeline from the exact revision.
7. Schedule publication and retain an audit trail. The proposed operating buffer is 14 approved daily racks, a planning target rather than an existing inventory.
8. Monitor reports and correct deliberately. Use a new revision, preserve historical attempts and avoid mixing split denominators across revisions.

## Review standards
Use the same rubric for every puzzle: decision clarity, legal setup, appropriate difficulty, defensible suggestion, useful alternative reasoning, clear captions and a clip that stays focused on one question. Objective rule violations can have a defined correct answer; strategic passes need a more nuanced outcome label.

## Corrections
A report records the puzzle/revision and the issue without publishing a reporter's private data. Staff may stop future submissions to a flawed revision, publish a reviewed correction and annotate affected results. Historical choices remain attached to their original version. Decide explicitly whether affected streaks receive credit; do not erase progress silently.

## Content fixtures
The included demo fixture and generated PNGs are not approved puzzles. The fixture has the intended tile IDs and face codes; the PNG art can vary. Teacher review and ruleset validation remain necessary.

## No creator outreach performed
The PRD proposes inviting teachers and posting daily clips. This package supplies a plan only. It has not contacted anyone, sent invites, registered a brand or posted content.
