## 1. CPI benchmarks for puzzle/casual games by platform and region (2024–2026), including mahjong/solitaire/tile-match and older-female audiences

### Takeaway
Blended casual CPIs are low: the Liftoff/Singular casual average for Feb 2024–Feb 2025 was $1.41 iOS and $0.14 Android, and Adjust's blended global gaming CPI for 2025 was $0.56, up 30%. ### Inferences

- Older-female audiences are typically reached most cheaply on Meta (Facebook). Supporting evidence: Facebook is Vita Mahjong's largest network (section 2). The higher Tier-1 CPMs are offset by this audience's long session times, which generate more ad impressions per user and so higher ad-LTV. This is inference; no CPI data split by age was found.
- Benchmarks disagree by up to 10x, because some use blended global installs including India/SEA (Liftoff $0.14 Android) and others use Tier-1 only. Always compare like-for-like geography.

## 2. Channel comparison for puzzle games; which channels dominate spend; minimum budgets and how small spenders start

### Takeaway
For gaming in 2025, Apple Ads ranks #1 on iOS globally and Google Ads #1 on Android, with AppLovin leading iOS gaming in Tier-1 North America and Western Europe (AppsFlyer Performance Index 2025). AppLovin's share has surged for puzzle advertisers in 2025–26. Meta is the largest network for Vita Mahjong, the clearest senior-targeted mahjong case. Small studios can start on Meta, Google and Apple Ads at tens of dollars a day. AppLovin needs MAX mediation for its ad-revenue ROAS campaigns and enough daily budget for 15–20 target events.

### Cited Findings
**Rankings and share**
- AppsFlyer Performance Index 2025 (released 3 Dec 2025; 16.2B non-organic installs, 39K apps, 88 media sources). (MMP, not a network.)
  - "Apple Ads remained the number one global media source for iOS gaming".
  - AppLovin led iOS gaming in Tier-1 North America and Western Europe.
  - Google Ads remained #1 for Android gaming.
  - AppLovin, Mintegral and adjoe (rewarded) posted major gains on Android gaming.
  - Meta and TikTok gained ground in general terms; no gaming rank was given for them.
  - Unity, ironSource, Moloco and Liftoff were not mentioned in the gaming headlines.
  — [AppsFlyer press release](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/performance-index-2025/)
- Sensor Tower, "Game Advertising in 2026" (summarized 22 Oct 2026):
  - AppLovin's share of impressions for Royal Match rose from 21.5% (Sep 2025) to 50.6% (Sep 2026), and for Royal Kingdom from 7.9% to 62.6%.
  - Gossip Harbor gets 81% of US impressions from AdMob, AppLovin and Liftoff combined.
  - Puzzle/merge titles get about 60% of impressions from in-app networks.
  - Facebook is Vita Mahjong's largest network at 27.6% of impressions (Sep 2026).

  — [GameDev Reports summary of Sensor Tower](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-game-advertising-in)

**AppLovin (AppDiscovery / Axon)**
- SuperScale guide for game advertisers (13 Oct 2026; vendor):
  - Three ROAS campaign types: Ad ROAS (ad-monetized games), IAP ROAS, and Blended ROAS (hybrid). Ad ROAS and Blended ROAS require MAX for all ad mediation. IAP ROAS can run with other mediation.
  - An MMP is required before any campaign runs.
  - AppLovin publishes no minimum spend. Budget should cover 15–20 target events per day, with one global budget per platform.
  - ROAS goals run on D7 or D28 windows; AppLovin suggests D28 first if value arrives late. An unrealistic goal can stop delivery. Changing the goal (in about 5% steps) moved spend more than changing the cap.
  - Lead creatives are 9:16 video of up to 60s, plus a playable showing the core loop.
  - With weak purchase signal, give a campaign 2–3 weeks with more countries and a lower goal.
  — [SuperScale: AppLovin Axon for advertisers](https://superscale.com/resources/applovin-axon-for-advertisers)
- AppLovin's consumer-brands (e-commerce/web) page says campaigns can launch from $10/day. — [AppLovin Ads consumer brands](https://www.applovin.com/en/consumer-brands)
- Third-party coverage of the Axon Ads Manager for web/e-commerce advertisers:
  - It opened on a referral basis around Oct 2025, and the referral requirement was removed around 22 June 2026; another source says 2 July 2026. The sources conflict on dates.
  - Agencies say the $10/day floor only switches a campaign on, not what it needs to learn, and some recommend about $1,000/day for 5 days.
  - This self-serve change concerns web/e-commerce advertisers. Game advertisers have used AppDiscovery dashboards for longer. No primary AppLovin documentation was verified.
  — [Sprites.ai AppLovin cost](https://www.sprites.ai/applovin-ads-cost); [NoticeMeSenpai](https://noticemesenpai.com/news/applovin-axon-7b-first-year-june-self-serve-window-2026/); [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/axon-applovin-ads-manager-setting-up-your-first-video-ad-campaign)

**Google App Campaigns**
- Widely repeated budget guidance (agencies; AppRadar 2021 attributes it to Google):
  - Daily budget at least 50x target CPI for install campaigns, at least 10x tCPA for in-app action campaigns, and 15x for engagement campaigns.
  - Start tCPA bids 20–30% above goal.
  - Change bids by no more than 20% at a time to avoid resetting learning.
  - Move to tROAS once there is enough post-install data.
  - Google's own help-center page was not verified.
  — [Admiral Media Google App Campaigns best practices](https://admiral.media/google-app-campaigns-best-practices/); [AppRadar budgeting](https://appradar.com/academy/ads-budgeting); [Megadigital 2026 guide](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-app-install-campaigns/)

**Meta (Advantage+ app campaigns)**
- WARC reports that Meta's Advantage+ is "delivering results close to pre-iOS 14.5 levels", at the cost of advertiser control. — [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/feed/results-or-control-the-new-dilemma-facing-metas-advertisers/7875)

Meta's "Opportunity Score" test (Feb 2025) claimed a 5% median cost-per-result decrease for adopters. — [Fraud Blocker](https://fraudblocker.com/articles/meta-advantage-plus-campaigns); [Social Media Today, Feb 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-advantage-plus-ad-updates-february-2025/739472/)

**Apple Ads**
- Games CPT averages about $4.02 (MobileAction 2025) and median US Games CPI $4.63 (AppTweak 2025). Search-match CTR is 5–15% (Admiral). See section 1. — [MobileAction](https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-ads-2026-benchmark-report/executive-summary/); [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- A suggested sequencing for a small studio:
  1. **Meta** first, to reach older women and test creative cheaply; Facebook is Vita Mahjong's top network.
  2. **Google App Campaigns**, for Android volume. Budget about 50x target CPI, so a $1.50 tCPI needs about $75/day.
  3. **Apple Ads**, on exact-match "mahjong" keywords and brand defense. CPTs are expensive, but searchers have high intent.
  4. **AppLovin Ad ROAS**, once the game uses MAX mediation, has some MMP-tracked ad-revenue history, and can fund 15–20 target events/day. AppLovin is where puzzle spend is shifting and where most ad revenue is mediated.
  5. **Mintegral, Unity/ironSource, Moloco and Liftoff** as later diversification.
- Ad-monetized games using MAX gain a structural advantage. AppLovin's Ad ROAS bidding can optimize on impression-level ad revenue that its own mediation sees, which is why it suits ad-LTV-heavy mahjong games. The Ad ROAS requirement for MAX is from SuperScale. The advantage itself is inference.
- TikTok's role for older-female mahjong audiences is likely smaller than for younger casual audiences. No mahjong-specific TikTok data was found.

### Gaps
- No public share-of-spend breakdown by network for the puzzle/mahjong genre specifically, beyond the individual titles above.
- No primary AppLovin documentation on AppDiscovery minimums for game advertisers was found. The $10/day figure is for e-commerce/web.
- No primary documentation on Meta's 2025 changes to Advantage+ app campaigns, or any gaming-specific results, was found.
- No Unity/ironSource, Moloco or Liftoff puzzle-specific performance data was found.

## 3. Creative strategy for mahjong/tile-match ads (themes, policy risk, volume, iteration, playables, AI)

### Takeaway
The proven mahjong playbook in 2024–2026 is Vita Mahjong's. It positions the game as "designed for seniors" with high-volume, AI-produced creatives featuring AI doctors, seniors and IQ/brain claims, and it scaled to roughly 400K downloads a day. Health claims have already drawn a UK ASA ruling against Vita Studio (Dec 2024), so brain-health and medical framing is a real regulatory risk. Across casual games, gameplay hooks dominate: "success" outcomes work better on ad networks and "fail" outcomes on social. Creative volume has exploded, with the median top US game advertiser launching about 465 new creatives a month in Q1 2026. Spend is extremely concentrated in a few winners, with the top 2% of creatives taking 53% of spend.

### Cited Findings
**Vita Mahjong case (category leader)**
- Gamigion (article dated 4 March, likely 2025):
  - Vita Mahjong passed $150K/day in ad revenue with about 400K downloads/day, and passed 65M downloads nearly a year after launch.
  - The core concept is "designed for seniors". Creatives feature AI-generated doctors and seniors claiming the game helps them, plus an Einstein-style figure claiming it raises IQ.
  - High-volume AI creative production is credited for the scale.
  - The product has minimal features (levels plus minor events) and is tuned for difficulty and funnels, Block Blast-style.
  — [Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/vita-mahjong-scaled-past-150k-a-day/)
- Sensor Tower: Vita Mahjong earned $89.8M in ad revenue Jan–May 2026, up more than 2x YoY. It drove most of the +$105M gain in the hypercasual "Match Pair" subgenre (about 7% of all game ad revenue). Tile Explorer added $20M. Hypercasual solitaire grew $64M, led by Solitaire Associations Journey ($73.3M, Jan–May 2026). (Sponsored summary.) — [GameDev Reports: Sensor Tower Ad Monetization 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- Sensor Tower's State of Gaming 2026 names puzzle and strategy as the hottest 2025 genres by revenue growth. It cites Block Blast! and Mahjong Vita for nailing session loops and making creative promises that "accurately represent the experience". — [GameDev Reports: Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- A Balancy analysis (June 2025) reports that Vita Mahjong delays interstitials until after Level 11 and keeps the "No Ads" purchase out of the way to avoid accidental purchases by seniors. Search-snippet level. — [Balancy](https://balancy.co/blog/2025/06/17/monetization-and-gameplay-analysis-of-top-downloaded-vita-mahjong/)
- An unverified single-blog claim says Vita Mahjong was the most heavily advertised game on iOS and Android for seven consecutive months and ran CTV campaigns. — [BreakEvenPointCalculator](https://breakevenpointcalculator.com/how-does-vita-mahjong-make-money-business-model-explained/)

**Policy and regulatory risk (independent: regulator)**
- UK ASA ruling against Vita Studio Pte Ltd, 4 Dec 2024:
  - The ad was an in-game ad for Vita Mahjong, seen 20 July 2024 inside Crossword Jam, featuring a man in blue medical scrubs.
  - It claimed the game was "designed Exclusively for people with memory loss and sleep loss" and that "30 minutes everyday… will greatly help you with memory and sleep".
  - The complaint was upheld under CAP Code 12.2 (discouraging essential treatment), 12.1, 3.1 (misleading) and 3.7 (substantiation; no clinical evidence).
  - The ad must not reappear. Vita Studio removed it and apologized.
  — [ASA ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/vita-studio-pte-ltd-g24-1253610-vita-studio-pte-ltd.html)
- The ASA upheld a complaint against Gorgeous Shard Puzzle Studio (17 Sep 2025) over an offensive ad showing a nude woman covered by tiles. Earlier, a 2024 Evony ruling found an ad showing puzzle gameplay misleading because the real game was a city-builder, which set the "fake gameplay" precedent. The ASA has published guidance on misleading gameplay in game ads. — [ASA Gorgeous Shard ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/gorgeous-shard-puzzle-studio-g25-1301129-gorgeous-shard-puzzle-studio.html); [ASA guidance "Don't hate the player…"](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/don-t-hate-the-player-hate-the-game-play-misleading-gameplay-in-videogame-ads.html); [Mishcon de Reya on misleading gameplay ruling](https://www.mishcon.com/news/asa-rules-mobile-game-ad-was-misleading-gameplay-depicted-did-not-reflect-the-core-game-playing-experience)
- User reviews of senior-targeted mahjong games show backlash when ads promise "no ads" or "free" but the game is ad-heavy. Example: "Main reason we downloaded game was that we advertised no ads and free. To our dismay it has ads." — [iOSAppLists Vita Mahjong review 2026](https://iosapplists.com/vita-mahjong-review-2026/)

**What works across casual games (AppsFlyer State of Creative Optimization 2025; Q1 2024–Q1 2025; 1.1M video creatives, 1.3K apps, $2.4B spend; MMP data, "Casual" includes Puzzle/Match/Tabletop)**
- The top 2% of gaming creatives took 53% of spend in Q1 2025, down from 56% in Q1 2024.
- Gaming apps spending $7M+ a quarter averaged 2,743 video variations per quarter. Top spenders ($1M+/quarter) grew creative volume about 8% YoY, while lower spenders shrank about 3%.
- Gameplay hooks dominate casual creative because they are cheap to produce (screen recordings).
- "Bold claims" drew high social spend but low IPM, meaning users tune them out. Humorous skits had high IPM on ad networks and DSPs despite modest spend.
- On ad networks, "Pure Success" outcomes got about 33% higher IPM. On Social & Search, "Pure Failure" got about 65% higher IPM.
- "Excitement" and "Completion" motivations take most of the budget. Excitement retains better at D7.
- For UGC, "Gameplay Reviews" outperform testimonials on IPM and retention, reaching 22% D7 on ad networks with under 5% of spend.
— [AppsFlyer Creative Optimization Report 2025](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/creative-optimization-report-2025/)

**Volume and format trends (Sensor Tower, 2026)**
- The median top US mobile game advertiser launched 135 new creatives a month in Q1'25 and 465 in Q1'26, about 400 a month since. Spend grew only about 1.7x, so each creative gets about half as much money.
- 70–75% of new creatives are video. Statics rose from 17 to 60 a month. Playables stayed under 15 a month even at peak.
- Sensor Tower links the volume growth to AI creative tools and to platforms rewarding volume.
— [GameDev Reports: Sensor Tower Game Advertising 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-game-advertising-in)
- AppLovin recommends 9:16 video of up to 60s plus a playable of the core loop, and auto-tests combinations within creative sets. — [SuperScale](https://superscale.com/resources/applovin-axon-for-advertisers)
- In Liftoff's report, Rovio's test of character order in the first 3 seconds (Angry Birds first, versus a pig) produced higher IPM, which shows the importance of hook testing. — [Liftoff 2025 Casual Gaming Apps Report](https://liftoff.ai/2025-casual-gaming-apps-report/)

**Store-listing messaging used by mahjong competitors (indicates the creative angles in market)**
- Large, easy-to-read tiles and senior positioning: Vita Mahjong, Tile Mahjong Classic. Brain-training claims ("improves focus, observation, memory"). "Relaxing / no timer". Ad-free positioning as a differentiator: "Mahjong Blast" ("100% ad free"), "Mahjong – No Forced Ads" (solvable levels, unlimited undo). Offline play. — [Mahjong – No Forced Ads](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-no-forced-ads/id6760566383); [Mahjong Blast](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-blast/id6747492600); [Tile Mahjong Classic](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sofish.tilemahjong.gp&hl=en_US); [Mahjong Match: Classic Tiles](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-match-classic-tiles/id6739490118)

### Inferences
- Recommended creative pillars for a small mahjong studio, in order of risk:
  1. Real, satisfying tile-clearing gameplay. Use "near-fail then clear" for Meta/TikTok and "clean success" for ad networks, per AppsFlyer's split.
  2. Relaxing/zen and large-tile accessibility messaging, framed in terms of comfort and readability rather than health claims.
  3. UGC-style gameplay reviews by older players.
  4. Light "brain workout" framing without medical or IQ claims. The ASA precedent shows "memory loss/sleep" claims and medical props (scrubs, doctors) are high risk, especially in the UK, and likely under platform policies too.
  5. Avoid fake gameplay. ASA precedent applies, and Sensor Tower credits Vita's success partly to creatives that match the game.
- Volume: a small studio cannot match 400+ creatives a month. With the top 2% of creatives taking more than half of spend, the practical approach is to test many cheap concepts (10–20 new variants a week using AI-assisted editing and screen-capture gameplay). Kill ads on early IPM/CPI and iterate on the winners' hooks (first 3 seconds). This is inference from the cited distributions.
- Playables are a low share of new creatives by count but are recommended by AppLovin. One good playable of the core matching loop is probably enough at small scale.

### Gaps
- No public creative-level IPM/CPI data specific to mahjong (for example from the Sensor Tower Creative Library or Meta Ad Library) was retrieved. The Meta Ad Library was not queried in this pass.
- No data on ASMR or "real physical tiles" creatives, video length, or AI-creative share for puzzle games specifically.
- No Liftoff creative report with puzzle-specific numbers was found.

## 4. Testing and scaling: marketability tests, soft launch markets, D7 ROAS targets, ROAS bidding, ad-LTV with AppLovin

### Takeaway
Industry practice is a staged approach:
1. A cheap CPI/IPM marketability test on Meta with store-page or prototype creatives.
2. A soft launch in 1–2 markets, one Tier-1 English market for a clean read and one cheap-volume market for retention.
3. Gating on D1/D7 retention and LTV:CPI before buying in the US.

ROAS targets vary widely by source. A reasonable vendor consensus for ad-monetized casual games is about 15–25% D7 ROAS and 40–60% D30, with Liftoff's casual D30 ROAS averages at 47% iOS and 15% Android. AppLovin's Ad ROAS campaigns, which require MAX, make ad-LTV directly biddable.

### Cited Findings
- Game Growth Advisor soft-launch guide (16 Dec 2025, updated 1 Aug 2026; consultant; CPI bands and thresholds are the author's judgement, retention benchmarks sourced):
  - Run two markets in parallel: one resembling the paying audience (UK/CA/AU at $1.00–2.50 Android CPI) and one for cheap volume (LATAM $0.15–0.60, SEA $0.20–0.60). Avoid the US, Japan, Korea and China in phase 1. iOS costs about 3–4x Android.
  - Lean run: 4 weeks, 1 market, about $11K–23K total ($9K–15K media). Full programme: 12 weeks, 2 markets, about $62K–110K. Media floor about $300–500/day per ad set.
  - Kill/extend criteria: D1 below 25% (Android) means stop buying; D7 below 8% means stop scaling; D30 below 3% means stop. CPI should be 30–70% of projected LTV. Scale test: triple spend; a CPI rise under 30% is a go, over 50% means saturated.
  - Cohorts of about 900 installs per test cell are needed to read D1 near 30% within ±3 points at 95% confidence.
  - Casual/puzzle D1 (Android) median is about 22% (GameAnalytics, 11.6K games), top quartile 25–27%. D7 median is 3.4–3.9%, top quartile 7–8%.
  - Google Play closed testing (12 testers for 14 days for new personal accounts) can add 3–5 weeks.
  - Paid UA at Tier-1 costs "starts to pay back around 18% D7" (stated for hybrid-casual).
  — [Game Growth Advisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2025-12-16-mobile-soft-launch-complete-guide/)
- FoxData (Apr 2026, citing Adjust and others): target LTV:CPI of at least 1.5x. Suggested D1 of 30%+ and D7 of 15–18% for competitive paid UA. Mature games reinvest about 20–40% of monthly revenue in UA. — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/2026-mobile-game-user-acquisition-cost-benchmarks-how-much-should-you-spend/)
- Vendor D7 ROAS targets conflict:
  - One source says 20–25% D7 and 50–70% D30. Another says 25–50% D7. A third says a casual, ad-monetized game might target 15–25% D7 and 40–60% D30.
  - These come from vendor blogs (Udonis, Hubapps, Admiral, Game Growth Advisor). Which page states which range was not verified beyond search snippets.
  — [Udonis casual games 2026](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/casual-games); [Hubapps ROAS guide](https://hubapps.team/blog/roas-guide-for-mobile-games); [Admiral 2026 benchmarks](https://admiral.media/mobile-app-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)
- Liftoff/Singular (Feb 2024–Feb 2025): average D30 ROAS for casual games is 47% iOS and 15% Android. — [Liftoff](https://liftoff.ai/2025-casual-gaming-apps-report/)
- Admiral (agency): D7 retention of 25%+ is top quartile, 15–20% is "industry average", and below 10% signals core-loop issues. This conflicts with GameAnalytics' much lower D7 medians, because the definitions and samples differ. — [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/mobile-game-marketing-benchmarks/)
- AppLovin bidding:
  - Ad ROAS / IAP ROAS / Blended ROAS campaigns run on D7 or D28 goals. Ad ROAS and Blended ROAS require MAX for all mediation.
  - Base the first goal on other channels' results. Change goals in about 5% steps.
  - For IAP ROAS, send every real purchase (including organic) and exclude ad revenue, predicted LTV and zero-value events.
  — [SuperScale](https://superscale.com/resources/applovin-axon-for-advertisers)
- Google App Campaigns: move from tCPI/tCPA to tROAS once enough post-install conversion data exists. — [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-app-install-campaigns/)

### Inferences
- A suggested small-studio test ladder:
  1. **Marketability test (pre-launch):** $500–2,000 on Meta, CA/AU/UK, optimizing for link clicks or installs on a placeholder store page. Compare 5–10 creative concepts on CTR, IPM and CPI.
  2. **Soft launch:** Android in CA/AU (plus the Philippines or Brazil for cheap retention reads). Gate on D1 ≥ 35–40% for a casual mahjong game. Category leaders likely exceed generic medians because the genre has high retention among older players; this is inference with no direct data.
  3. **iOS + US scale-up**, once D7 ROAS meets the payback curve implied by the D30/D90 multiplier.
- Ad-monetized mahjong games realize LTV slowly and steadily from daily sessions, so D28 goals (AppLovin) or D30+ payback windows suit them better than D7. Long-retention senior audiences can pay back over 90–180 days.
- Canada and Australia remain the standard Tier-1 English soft-launch proxies. The Philippines is a common cheap English-speaking market, though no Philippines-specific CPI was found.

### Gaps
- No public mahjong-specific retention or ROAS curves were found.
- No authoritative, non-vendor D7 ROAS target was found. All targets are rules of thumb.
- No data on the ROAS uplift from AppLovin Ad ROAS versus CPI campaigns for ad-monetized puzzle games.

## 5. Measurement: ATT opt-in, SKAN 4 / AdAttributionKit, Android Privacy Sandbox status, MMPs and costs for small studios

### Takeaway
Gaming ATT opt-in among users shown the prompt is about 38–39% (Adjust, Q1 2025 / Q1 2026) and about 32% in the US. SKAN 4 reached majority adoption only in 2026, and AdAttributionKit production adoption is still early. Google retired most Privacy Sandbox technologies (Attribution Reporting API, Protected Audience, Topics) on 17 Oct 2025, so Android attribution continues to rely on GAID and MMP methods. Small studios can start with free MMP tiers: Tenjin free up to 2,000 conversions a month, and AppsFlyer with 12,000 free conversions in the first 12 months.

### Cited Findings
- Adjust ATT opt-in (shown-prompt basis; vendor MMP data):
  - Gaming was 37.9% in Q1 2025 (37.5% a year earlier). The US was about 32%, flat; Indonesia led at 58.6%.
  - The industry-wide average was 35% in Q2 2025.
  - In Q1 2026 the industry was 38% (35% in Q1 2025), and gaming was the highest category at 39%.
  — [Adjust ATT opt-in rates 2025](https://www.adjust.com/blog/att-opt-in-rates-2025); [PPC Land on Adjust 2026 report](https://ppc.land/adjusts-2026-mobile-app-report-finance-sessions-up-21-gaming-cpi-jumps-30/)
- SKAN/AAK:
  - A Kochava piece says SKAN 4 was "finally reaching majority adoption" as of mid-2026. Its survey found 33% of respondents had not implemented SKAN at all.
  - A Singular webinar Q&A (2026) says AdAttributionKit adoption is "still very early… not seeing meaningful production adoption today".
  - Apple's 2025 AAK updates (iOS 18.4 / iOS 26) added overlapping re-engagement windows, configurable attribution rules and geography in anonymous postbacks. SKAN and AAK remain interoperable.
  - SKAN 4 install windows are days 0–2, 3–7 and 8–35.
  — [Kochava](https://www.kochava.com/blog/your-ios-attribution-strategy-2026-reality-check/); [Singular webinar Q&A](https://www.singular.net/blog/scaling-ios-apps-in-2026-webinar-qa/); [Adjust AAK vs SKAN](https://www.adjust.com/blog/adattributionkit/); [Apple WWDC24 Meet AdAttributionKit](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10060/)
- Privacy Sandbox:
  - Google announced on 17 Oct 2025 that it would retire most Privacy Sandbox technologies (Attribution Reporting API, Protected Audience, Topics) citing low adoption. The phase-out follows standard Chrome and Android deprecation processes, and the brand is dropped. CHIPS is retained.
  - The UK CMA released Google from its commitments days earlier.
  — [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/10/20/google-is-retiring-privacy-sandbox/); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-kills-privacy-sandbox-3608497); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chrome-kills-most-privacy-sandbox-technologies-after-adoption-fails/)
- MMP costs:
  - AppsFlyer: the "Zero" plan is free but meant for owned media, "not suitable for apps with paid activities". Zero and Growth include a 12,000-conversion welcome package to use within 12 months. Growth is then pay-as-you-go at about $0.07 per conversion. Organic installs are free and not counted.
  — [AppsFlyer pricing](https://www.appsflyer.com/pricing/); [Airbridge comparison](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/appsflyer-vs-airbridge-pricing)
  - Tenjin: the free plan is $0/month with 2,000 free conversions a month, then $0.04 per conversion, with no contract. Paid plans start at $200/month.
  — [Tenjin pricing](https://tenjin.com/pricing)
- AppLovin requires an MMP before any campaign runs. — [SuperScale](https://superscale.com/resources/applovin-axon-for-advertisers)

### Inferences
- For a small mahjong studio:
  - **MMP:** start with Tenjin (free tier, which also aggregates ad revenue and cost data for indies) or AppsFlyer's 12K-conversion welcome package. Budget about $0.04–0.07 per paid install after the free allowance.
  - **ATT:** older audiences may opt in at different rates; no data was found. Plan iOS optimization around SKAN 4 coarse/fine values plus network-modeled conversions (Meta AEM, AppLovin).
  - **Android:** with Privacy Sandbox retired, Android remains the more measurable platform. That supports Android-first soft launches and ROAS bidding.

### Gaps
- No Adjust or Singular indie pricing was found; both typically require sales contact.
- No ATT opt-in rate by age group or for puzzle/mahjong specifically.
- No primary Apple documentation on AAK in iOS 26 was verified.
- Whether Google has fully removed the Android Attribution Reporting API from devices as of Oct 2026 was not verified beyond the Oct 2025 announcement.

## 6. ASO for mahjong: keywords, title/subtitle, creatives, ratings, localization, custom pages, in-app events, featuring, organic vs paid share

### Takeaway
Mahjong store listings are dominated by keyword-stacked titles: "Mahjong" plus Solitaire / Tile / Match / Classic, plus accessibility hooks such as "large tiles", "for seniors" and "no ads". Apple's July 2025 change lets custom product pages (now up to 70) appear in organic search for assigned keywords. Google Play allows up to 50 custom store listings, and Google claims a median 10% conversion lift when CSLs are used with Google Ads. No public data on organic versus paid install share for top mahjong titles was found.

### Cited Findings
**Platform mechanics**
- iOS: title and subtitle are 30 characters each. The title is weighted most, then the subtitle. The keyword field is 100 characters (single words, comma-separated, singular forms, no competitor brands). The long description is not indexed on iOS. Repeating keywords across fields gives no extra weight. — [AppTweak iOS keyword field](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/how-to-optimize-your-ios-keyword-field); [AppTweak keyword research 2026](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/app-store-keyword-research-aso)
- Google Play: AppTweak material says keyword density (2–3%) in the description is a ranking signal. The same material cites a 50-character title. That conflicts with Google's 2021 policy, which limited app titles to 30 characters; that policy was not re-verified in this research. — [AppTweak keyword research](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/app-store-keyword-research-aso)
- Apple custom product pages (CPPs) in organic search:
  - Announced at WWDC25 and live around 30 July 2025.
  - Developers assign keywords from the keyword field (not the title or subtitle) to a CPP. Each keyword combination maps to one CPP. App Store Connect reports search impressions per CPP.
  - The CPP limit rose from 35 to 70 per app around 29 Oct 2025 (per Adapty). Deep links let a CPP open a specific in-app destination (iOS 18+).
  - Vendor blogs; not verified against Apple's docs.
  — [Adapty CPP guide](https://adapty.io/blog/custom-product-pages-app-store/); [Phiture](https://phiture.com/blog/keyword-based-custom-product-pages-cpps-arrive-in-app-store-connect/); [AppTweak CPP guide](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-tools/custom-product-pages-cpp?lang=en-US)
- Google Play custom store listings (CSLs):
  - Up to 50 CSLs, each with its own name, icon, descriptions and graphics, targetable by country, inactive users, or Google Ads campaign. Custom URLs are supported. Store listing experiments run per listing.
  - Google (PlayTime, its own figures): CSLs used in Google Ads campaigns gave a median 10% conversion lift. Portrait videos gave +7% watch time, +9% completions and +5% conversions.
  - An unverified 2026 guide says Play Console will judge listings by CTR from July 2026.
  — [AppTweak PlayTime 2025 recap](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/google-playtime-recap-google-plays-vision-for-2025); [ASOMobile CSL guide](https://asomobile.net/en/blog/custom-store-listing-in-google-play/)

**Mahjong keyword/title landscape (observed store listings)**
- Typical title patterns:
  - "Vita Mahjong" (senior positioning in the description)
  - "Mahjong Match：Classic Tiles!"
  - "Daily Mahjong Match"
  - "Tile Mahjong Classic"
  - "Arcadia Mahjong"
  - "Mahjong Blast"
  - "Mahjong - No Forced Ads"
  These show stacking of "mahjong" with match / tile / classic / daily, plus differentiators such as "no ads".
  — [Mahjong Match](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-match-classic-tiles/id6739490118); [Daily Mahjong Match](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daily-mahjong-match/id6741703805); [Tile Mahjong Classic](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sofish.tilemahjong.gp&hl=en_US); [Arcadia Mahjong](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/arcadia-mahjong/id6503700976); [Mahjong – No Forced Ads](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-no-forced-ads/id6760566383)
- American mahjong niche:
  - Real Mah Jongg (Mattron Entertainment, since 2017; not the NMJL's official app) has 50K+ Google Play downloads and a 4.4★ rating from about 800 reviews. It plays the current card ("Play with the 2026 card") and costs about $6.99/month after a 2-week free trial.
  - Alternatives include Mahjong 4 Friends (free with ads) and MahJongg4Fun (free, optional membership).
  - Several 2026 blogs rank "best American mahjong apps", which shows search demand from that community.
  — [Real Mah Jongg on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.realmahjongg&hl=en_US); [Bam Good Time: best American mahjong apps 2026](https://bamgoodtime.com/blog/best-american-mahjong-apps-2026); [Mahjong 4 Friends guide](https://mahjong4friends.com/guides/best-mahjong-app)
- User-review pain points to address in listings and screenshots: ad frequency, hard-to-close ads, rewarded ads that fail to deliver, and privacy labels listing tracking data for a game "pitched at older players". — [iOSAppLists Vita Mahjong review 2026](https://iosapplists.com/vita-mahjong-review-2026/)

### Inferences
- Suggested iOS metadata structure:
  - **Title:** "Mahjong Solitaire: [Brand]", or "[Brand] Mahjong – Tile Match", leading with the highest-volume head term "mahjong".
  - **Subtitle:** secondary terms such as "Classic Tile Match Puzzle" or "Big Tiles, Relaxing Games".
  - **Keyword field:** long-tail terms (free, classic, solitaire, tile, match, puzzle, brain, relax, seniors, offline, zen, shanghai, connect, pair, american, riichi), not repeated from the title or subtitle.
  - **Keyword-assigned CPPs:** e.g. "american mahjong", "mahjong for seniors / large tiles", "relaxing zen tiles".
- Screenshots for an older audience: large-tile close-ups, readable captions (large fonts), "No timer / Unlimited undo / Play offline" benefit callouts, and a portrait gameplay video.
- Ratings matter disproportionately in a crowded, commoditized genre. Prompt for ratings after level wins, and avoid aggressive ads early (Vita delays interstitials to after Level 11).
- Localization: top secondary markets for mahjong solitaire likely include DE, FR, JP, BR-PT and ES. No market-share data was found, so this is inference.
- American mahjong (NMJL card-based rules) and riichi are separate niches with different keyword intents. "Mahjong solitaire / tile match" players and "American mahjong" players are different audiences. An American-mahjong product competes with subscription apps such as Real Mah Jongg.

## 7. Organic and low-cost channels: cross-promotion, communities, YouTube/TikTok organic, web portals (CrazyGames, Poki, Facebook Instant Games)

### Takeaway
Web portals offer low-cost, data-generating funnels. CrazyGames data shows mahjong is popular, with a US audience skewing female and 55+. Poki and CrazyGames both run staged soft-release tests, and CrazyGames added a mobile app and IAP in 2025. Owned cross-promotion lifts ATT opt-in and has driven hyper-casual organic growth. No hard data was found on Facebook-group or American-mahjong-club community marketing, or on Facebook Instant Games as a funnel.

### Cited Findings
- CrazyGames data (via a promotional press release, Sept 2025) shows a surge in mahjong popularity by country and a female-skewed US player base, especially women over 55. — [IPS News / DiscoveryContent](https://ipsnews.net/business/2025/09/09/surge-in-mahjong-popularity-a-country-level-breakdown/)
- Poki: staged Player Fit Test, then Web Fit Test (3–7 days; sources conflict), then review, then about 2–3 weeks of soft release before global release. Poki Playtesting can send up to 50 players per playtest for free. Poki says it does not sell placement. (Third-party guides.) — [Cinevva guide to Poki (2026)](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki); [Playgama FAQ](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/how-to-get-a-web-game-featured-on-poki-and-crazygames/)

  - QA, then "Basic Launch" (soft launch to a small player segment), then possible Full Launch with SDK and homepage placement.
  - In 2025 CrazyGames added soft-launch tools, performance insights and in-app purchases. It launched a mobile app with "hundreds of thousands" of organic downloads and scaled 20+ games across web and mobile through publishing.

  — [Playgama FAQ](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/how-to-get-a-web-game-featured-on-poki-and-crazygames/); [LinkedIn CrazyGames 2025 recap](https://tr.linkedin.com/in/yagizefemertol)
