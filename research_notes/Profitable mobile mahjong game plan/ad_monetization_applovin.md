## 1. AppLovin's product suite, its exit from games, market position and controversies

### Takeaway
AppLovin is now a pure ad-tech company. It sold its first-party games to Tripledot (closed 30 June 2025) and shut down Array in 2025. What remains:
- **MAX**: mediation, free to publishers.
- **AppLovin Exchange (ALX)**: its programmatic exchange.
- **AppLovin Ads, rebranded "Axon"**: AI-driven ads for user acquisition (UA), with self-serve Ads Manager open to all advertisers from June 2026.
- **Adjust**: its mobile measurement partner (MMP).

AppLovin is the largest ad network in mobile games (about 36% of game ad revenue from January 2025 to May 2026, per Sensor Tower). MAX is the largest mediation platform (over 50% share). It is very profitable (FY2025 revenue $5.48B, adjusted EBITDA margin above 80%). It also faces an ongoing SEC probe of its data-collection practices, which followed 2025 short-seller reports.

### Inferences
- Selling the studios removes the most obvious conflict of interest: AppLovin no longer competes with publishers like a mahjong developer for players or in the store. Its UA buyers (Tripledot, among others) still benefit from MAX auction data, and AppLovin still both runs the auction (MAX) and bids in it (AppLovin Ads/ALX). "Neutrality" is better than before 2025 but not complete.
- The move of Axon into e-commerce should bring more non-game advertiser demand into game ad inventory. That is plausibly good for eCPMs on older US audiences, who are attractive to retail advertisers. This is an inference; no source quantifies it.
- For a small team the regulatory risk is mainly reputational and continuity risk, not operational. MAX is still where most game ad revenue flows. A practical hedge: keep the AdMob/Google relationship active (as a MAX bidder, or as a fallback mediator) so that switching mediators is possible.

## 2. Mediation platforms compared: AppLovin MAX vs Unity LevelPlay vs Google AdMob

### Takeaway
MAX leads clearly (about 73% of top-downloaded and 55% of top-grossing ad-monetized games in a May 2025 sample). LevelPlay has lost ground. AdMob is the main alternative and is closing the gap. The three together hold over 90% of the market.
- **Fees**: MAX mediation is free to publishers (AppLovin charges bidders instead). No fee terms were found on the LevelPlay or AdMob pages fetched.
- **Engine support**:
  - MAX has an official Godot plugin plus Unity support.
  - AdMob has official Unity and Flutter SDKs; Godot support is community-made.
  - LevelPlay is Unity-first.

### Cited Findings
**Market share** (GameBiz Consulting, May 2025; sample of 368 games from 217 publishers, AppMagic top-200 grossing and top-200 downloaded, March 2025):
- MAX was used in 128 of 175 top-downloaded games with ads (73.1%) and 33 of 60 top-grossing games with ads (55%), more than all other platforms combined.
- AdMob's share among top-downloaded games was more than double LevelPlay's.
- MAX + LevelPlay + AdMob hold over 90% of the market. FairBid, Appodeal, Loomit and Chartboost together hold under 10%.
- In 2022 MAX and ironSource were roughly equal.
- [independent consultant] — [PocketGamer.biz, 3 Jun 2025](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nine-facts-about-the-mediation-space-in-2025/)

**Other claims about MAX**
- One secondary source claims AppLovin mediation has "more than 80% market share." This conflicts with GameBiz's "more than half." [secondary] — [Segwise, Jun 2026](https://segwise.ai/blog/applovin-publisher-monetization-guide)
- Weaknesses listed by GameBiz (May 2025):
  - Multiple ad units and 5-second banner refresh were introduced, then discouraged or restricted.
  - Ad quality tools lag LevelPlay.
  - No fill-rate or latency metrics.
  - Direct deals, landscape creatives and COPPA (child-directed) support have been deprecated.
  - Some reports disappear after 2–4 weeks.
  - Ads cannot be blocked from the dashboard.
  - As of 16 July (2025), networks with SDK bidders must run in bidding-only mode in MAX, with few exceptions (for example HyprMX).
  - [independent consultant] — [PocketGamer.biz, 3 Jun 2025](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nine-facts-about-the-mediation-space-in-2025/)
  - This partly conflicts with AppLovin's own claim that "Ad Review gives publishers visibility and control over which ads users see." [vendor] — [AppLovin Monetization page](https://applovin.com/monetization)
- **Integration effort**:

It says publishers under 50K DAU "rarely generate enough data." [secondary] — [Segwise, Jun 2026](https://segwise.ai/blog/applovin-publisher-monetization-guide)

  - One user review complains that "take rates are high." [anecdotal] — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/applovin-max)

### Inferences
- **For a small mahjong team in Godot**: MAX is the easiest technical fit (official plugin) and the strongest default. AdMob/Google and Meta, Unity, Mintegral, Moloco and others can all bid inside MAX. Choosing MAX also unlocks AppLovin ROAS campaigns later.
- **For a team in Unity**: MAX and LevelPlay are both one-click. The decision is mostly "AppLovin demand and UA (MAX)" vs "Unity/ironSource demand and better mediation UX (LevelPlay)." AppLovin does not bid on LevelPlay, so leaving MAX means losing AppLovin's top-ranked demand.
- **For a team in Flutter**: AdMob's first-party Flutter plugin is the lowest-risk path. AppLovin MAX can be a bidder in AdMob, but GameBiz says it underperforms there.
- Below about 50K DAU, sophisticated A/B tests and waterfall tuning have low statistical power. A small team should launch bidding-only with a handful of bidders and run few, large tests.

### Gaps
- No primary source found confirming LevelPlay's or AdMob's mediation fee. They are widely described as free, but this was not verified on the pages fetched.
- Official Flutter or Godot support for LevelPlay was not confirmed. An official MAX Flutter plugin was not confirmed (only a community repository surfaced).
- No independent, recent (2026) head-to-head study of revenue lift by mediation platform was found. Case studies are vendor-published.

## 3. Ad networks and bidders that matter for casual and puzzle games

### Takeaway
Four sources of demand matter most:
- AppLovin (about 36% of game ad revenue)
- Google AdMob/AdX (about 29%)
- Unity Ads plus ironSource Ads
- Mintegral

Next tier: Meta Audience Network, Moloco, Liftoff/Vungle, InMobi, Pangle, DT Exchange, BidMachine. Most of these now take part through in-app bidding rather than waterfalls. The networks available as bidders differ by mediator; in particular, AppLovin is not on LevelPlay.

### Inferences
Suggested bidder set at launch for a mahjong game on MAX:
- AppLovin
- Google bidding (AdMob/AdX)
- Unity Ads
- ironSource Ads
- Meta Audience Network (strong for older US/Facebook-heavy audiences; inference)
- Mintegral
- Moloco
- Liftoff/Vungle
- InMobi
- Pangle (strongest in Asia)
- DT Exchange and BidMachine (incremental programmatic demand)

Each additional SDK adds app size and maintenance. Start with the top 6–8 and add more only through A/B tests.

The Tripledot (+20% ARPDAU from adding bidders) and Me2Zen (+10–15%) results suggest auction density matters, but both are vendor-published.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 independent per-network share for Meta Audience Network, Moloco, Liftoff, InMobi, Pangle, DT Exchange, BidMachine or Chartboost was found.
- The current bidder lists inside MAX and LevelPlay were not retrieved from primary docs. Chartboost's status was flagged as "possibly no longer active" as a mediator by GameBiz.

## 4. Ad formats and placement design for puzzle games (frequency, timing, older audiences)

### Takeaway
Puzzle and match games tolerate ads well:
- Puzzle is 53% of all mobile game ad revenue (Sensor Tower, 2026).
- Appodeal's experts say puzzle and match games "can handle frequent interstitial and banner ads."

The proven stack:
- Opt-in rewarded video tied to genuine helpers (hint, shuffle, undo, extra time or lives, doubled coins).
- Interstitials at natural breaks, after the level-complete screen, with a time cooldown (about 60–120 s is the range in the guidance found).
- A persistent banner or MREC where it does not cover the board.

Adding interstitials to a rewarded-plus-banner setup raised ARPDAU about 18% at a cost of 2.5% D7 retention in one (2019) A/B test. No hard data was found specifically on older players' tolerance for interstitials.

### Cited Findings
- **Puzzle's share**: puzzle accounts for 53% of mobile game ad revenue. The largest sub-genres are Block (10%) and Match Pair, Sort and Match Swap (about 7% each). [independent] — [Sensor Tower via GameDev Reports, Jul 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- **Mahjong titles near the top**:
  - Top games by ad revenue, Jan–May 2026: Block Blast! $127M; **Vita Mahjong $89.8M**; Candy Crush Saga $81.1M; Solitaire Associations Journey $73.3M; Wordscapes $42.6M. [independent model estimate] — [Sensor Tower via GameDev Reports, Jul 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
  - Vita Mahjong (Vita Studio) markets itself as designed for seniors, contains ads, and sells an ad-removal option for about 31 zł (around $8).   - Meta's Instant Games guidance suggests 90–120 seconds between interstitials for mid-core and puzzle games, and enforces a 30-second minimum. [vendor] — [Meta Instant Games docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/games/monetize/in-app-ads/interstitial-ads)

  - 42% play more than an hour a day.
  - Top motivations: fun 46%, relaxing 45%, passing time 40%, "keep brain sharp" 39%.
  - Preferred genres: card, board, casino, match-3.
  - 52% are at least somewhat likely to engage with in-game rewards.
  - "Later in the day" is the best engagement window.
  - Cites AARP (2023): over 52M Americans over 50 play mobile games.

### Inferences
Mahjong placement blueprint, synthesized from the sources above (not a tested result):
- **Rewarded video (core format)**:
  - Placements: "Hint" (highlight a match), "Shuffle," "Undo," "+1 life / continue," "Double coins on level complete," a daily-bonus multiplier, and unlocking a themed tile set temporarily.
  - Make them opt-in, clearly labelled, with the reward granted immediately.
  - Rewarded impressions per DAU are the main lever for older players, who respond to rewards (52% are likely to engage).
- **Interstitials**:
  - Show only after the level-complete screen, never mid-board and never right after a failure.
  - Skip the first session or first ~5 levels.
  - Use a cooldown of at least 60–120 s.
  - Skip the interstitial if the user just watched a rewarded ad.
  - Run a holdout test with D1/D7/D30 retention as a guardrail.
  - The ZiMAD result (+18% ARPDAU for −2.5% D7) suggests the trade-off usually favours including interstitials. Validate it on your own cohort.
- **Banner / MREC**:
  - An adaptive banner is acceptable outside the play area.
  - On small phones, a banner may force tiles smaller, which hurts older players' readability. Consider dropping the banner during play and using an MREC on menu or level-complete screens instead.
- **App open ads**: possibly useful on cold start, but risky with older users who expect to resume quickly. Test cautiously.
- **Playables/native**: these are creative types that networks serve inside interstitial or rewarded slots, not placements you design.

### Gaps
- No public data was found on older players' interstitial tolerance or retention sensitivity specifically.
- No mahjong-specific ad-placement case study with numbers was found. The closest are solitaire (Tripledot, Me2Zen) and jigsaw (Veraxen) examples.
- No public evidence was found comparing rewarded interstitial with standard interstitial in puzzle games.

## 6. Hybrid monetization: ad removal, IAP versus ads, and the ad-revenue ROAS loop

### Takeaway
Puzzle and solitaire games are typically hybrid: ads for the majority, IAP (including "Remove Ads") for engaged players. In mature markets IAP is 77–90% of total game revenue, but ad-supported puzzle games skew much more toward ads. The AppLovin ecosystem creates a loop: MAX reports impression-level ad revenue to the MMP, ROAS campaigns are available only to MAX users, and ad revenue therefore directly funds and optimizes AppLovin UA.

### Cited Findings
- **Market splits**:
  - Ads are about 55–70% of game revenue in India, Indonesia and Brazil, while IAP is 77–90% in the US, Canada, South Korea and Japan.
  - Games outside the top 1,000 by revenue take 29% of ad revenue but only 9% of IAP. Long-tail games lean on ads.
  - The share of games with ad monetization rose from 45% (mid-2021) to 56% (May 2026).
  - [independent] — [Sensor Tower via GameDev Reports, Jul 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- **Remove-ads pattern**: a common hybrid design shows interstitials and banners to everyone, then offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase after several sessions. Buyers become targetable payers. [vendor] — [Meta monetization best practices](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/games/monetize/best-practices)
- **Hybrid-casual puzzle example**: ad-removal offers and fail-level offers bring in about 30% of monthly revenue combined (AppMagic, Q2 2025). [independent] — [AppMagic via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-top-10-hybrid-casual-games)
- **Genre guidance**: casual and puzzle games typically use a hybrid model because engaged players will pay to remove ads. [vendor] — [Unity monetization guide](https://unity.com/en/resources/mobile-game-monetization-guide)
- **Mahjong and senior-focused examples**:
  - Vita Mahjong sells ad removal (about 31 zł) plus IAP from about 4.79 zł to 239.99 zł. [secondary] — [instalki.pl](https://www.instalki.pl/news/software/vita-mahjong-nie-tylko-dla-seniorow-recenzja-gry/)
  - Its sister app Vita Solitaire for Seniors is estimated at 68% IAP / 32% ads. This is a third-party estimate for a different title. [secondary] — [AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/com.vitastudio.solitaire)
  - Sensor Tower estimates Vita Mahjong's ad revenue at $89.8M in Jan–May 2026, implying ads are a major revenue line for it. [independent] — [Sensor Tower via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- **Board, solitaire and word games** were reported to have a higher ad share of revenue than other genres (Tenjin commentary). This was seen only in a search snippet and not verified. — [Tenjin](https://tenjin.com/?p=11918)
- **The ROAS loop**:
  - AppLovin restricts ROAS campaigns to MAX users. [independent consultant] — [PocketGamer.biz, Jun 2025](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nine-facts-about-the-mediation-space-in-2025/)
  - MAX provides user- and impression-level ad revenue data and integrates with MMPs (Adjust, AppsFlyer, Singular, Tenjin and others). [vendor] — [AppLovin](https://applovin.com/monetization)
  - Unity likewise says LevelPlay publishers get the best results from Unity Ads and ironSource Ads ROAS campaigns. [vendor] — [Unity LevelPlay](https://unity.com/products/levelplay)
  - Unity Vector reportedly lifts installs and IAP by 15–20%. [independent consultant] — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nine-facts-about-the-mediation-space-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- **Recommended hybrid for mahjong**:
  1. Ads for everyone, with rewarded ads as the main "helper" economy.
  2. A one-time "Remove Ads" IAP (about $4.99–9.99; Vita charges about $8) that removes interstitials and banners but keeps optional rewarded ads, which players still like.
  3. Small coin, hint and booster packs.
  4. Optionally a "VIP / no-ads" subscription. No public data on its uptake in mahjong was found.
- Remove-ads buyers lose their ad ARPDAU. Price ad removal above the expected lifetime ad revenue of an engaged user. For example, $0.08/day × 180 days ≈ $14 for a heavily engaged US user; this is an inference from the model in section 5.
- **The loop for a small team**:
  1. Integrate MAX and an MMP (Adjust or another) with ad-revenue postbacks.
  2. Measure D7/D30 ROAS including ad revenue.
  3. Once there are enough installs and revenue events, run AppLovin (Axon) ROAS campaigns.
  4. Ad revenue from users acquired this way funds more UA.

  AppLovin earns twice: as UA seller and as mediator/exchange. The dependency risk is concentration in one vendor.

### Gaps
- No public data was found on "no ads" subscription adoption in puzzle games, or on cannibalization of ad revenue by remove-ads purchases.
- The minimum spend and data thresholds for AppLovin ROAS campaigns were not found.
- Adjust pricing for small studios was not researched.

## 7. Practical setup: consent (UMP/CMP), app-ads.txt, SKAdNetwork, test modes, payments

### Takeaway
Before launch:
1. Use a Google-certified CMP for EEA, UK and Switzerland traffic (Google's UMP is free and optional; any certified CMP works). Without one, Google serves only "Limited Ads." List every mediation partner in the consent message.
2. Publish app-ads.txt at the root of the developer website named in the store listing.
3. Add every network's SKAdNetwork IDs to Info.plist.
4. Initialize the mediation SDK early.
5. Test with the mediation debugger or AdMob ad inspector.
6. Submit tax forms.

AppLovin pays monthly at NET 15 once balances reach $100. Other mediated networks pay separately.

### Cited Findings
- **Certified CMP deadline**: "To avoid impact on monetization for users in the EEA and UK, you must implement a Google-certified CMP by January 16, 2024." Without one, only Limited Ads serve. Enforcement ramped up to all EEA/UK traffic by the end of February 2024. [vendor] — [AdMob Android GDPR guide](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/gdpr); [AdMob Flutter GDPR guide](https://developers.google.com/admob/flutter/privacy/gdpr)
- **Switzerland**: from 31 July 2024, partners must also use a certified, TCF-integrated CMP for Swiss users (per Google's announcement as summarized in search results). [vendor] — [AdMob announcement](https://support.google.com/admob/announcements/9175069)
- **CMP details** [vendor] — [AdMob Android GDPR guide](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/gdpr):
  - The UMP SDK is optional; any Google-certified CMP may be used.
  - Mediation partners must be added to the ad-partners list in the GDPR message. "Failure to do so can lead to partners failing to serve ads." Third-party CMPs must include each mediation partner as an ad-tech provider.
  - Both purpose consent and vendor consent are required.
  - Consent revocation must be available at any time.
  - Under-age handling uses `setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent` and `ageRestrictedTreatment`.
  - Debug privacy settings with the "Troubleshoot privacy settings" feature in ad inspector.
  - US state privacy laws are covered in a separate AdMob guide (not retrieved).
- **app-ads.txt (AdMob)** [vendor] — [AdMob Help: app-ads.txt](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9363762):
  - Host the file at the root of the developer website listed in your Google Play or App Store listing (App Store: marketing URL).
  - Format: `seller domain, publisher ID, relationship`, for example `google.com, pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX, DIRECT`. Follow the IAB Tech Lab spec.
  - Detection and crawling each take up to 24 hours. Firebase Hosting is an option if your site cannot host root files.
- **SKAdNetwork and initialization (MAX/Godot)**: add the SKAdNetwork identifiers to Info.plist for iOS 14+, initialize the SDK at app start so mediated networks have time to cache ads, and obtain consent and pass consent values to AppLovin where required. [vendor] — [AppLovin Godot docs](https://support.applovin.com/en/godot/overview/integration)
- **Testing**: AppLovin cites its Mediation Debugger and SDK validation tools for integration. [vendor] — [AppLovin](https://applovin.com/monetization)
- **AppLovin payment terms** [vendor] — [AppLovin Monetization Payment FAQ](https://support.applovin.com/en/max/faq/faq-applovin-monetization-payment):
  - $100 minimum threshold on AppLovin-network earnings; balances below it carry forward.
  - Paid on or around the 15th of the following month (NET 15).
  - Methods: ACH, wire, check or PayPal. Some methods carry fees; there are no AppLovin-side bank fees on USD.
  - A W-9 or W-8BEN(-E) tax form is required before any payment.
  - AppLovin pays for its own Ads Manager demand and ALX. Other mediated networks pay you directly. Google pays for AppLovin impressions served via Open Bidding.
- **ATT**: the opt-in rate was 38% in Q1 2026 (gaming 39%), so most iOS users are non-consented. Tenjin advises raising ATT opt-in to boost iOS ad revenue. [independent] — [Adjust via Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026); [Tenjin via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/tenjin-ad-monetization-in-mobile)

### Inferences
Practical launch checklist for a small mahjong team (synthesized):
1. Create a MAX account and app; create separate ad units per platform and format (rewarded, interstitial, banner/MREC).
2. Integrate the MAX SDK (official Godot or Unity plugin) and 6–8 bidder adapters.
3. Implement a certified CMP: Google UMP inside MAX's flow, or MAX's own CMP integration (not researched). Include all partners.
4. Implement the iOS ATT prompt with a pre-prompt explainer.
5. Add every network's SKAdNetwork IDs to Info.plist.
6. Publish app-ads.txt with lines for each network (AppLovin, Google and others) on the developer website.
7. Enable test mode and test devices; verify every network loads in the Mediation Debugger.
8. Connect the MMP with ad-revenue postbacks.
9. Submit tax forms and payment details with each network that pays directly (AppLovin, Google, Unity, Meta and others).

Expect separate payouts and thresholds per network, and a cash lag of about 1.5 months (NET 15 for AppLovin).
