## 1. What are the distinct mahjong sub-genres on mobile, and how do they differ in audience and economics?

### Takeaway
"Mahjong" on mobile is really four businesses with almost nothing in common. (a) Mahjong solitaire (Shanghai-style pair matching) is a mass-market, ad-funded casual genre that analytics firms file under "Hypercasual Match Pair", and Vita Mahjong now dominates it. (b) 3-tile "triple match" derivatives are an IAP-led puzzle sub-genre that is shrinking. (c) 4-player Riichi is a Japan-centric, gacha/IAP business led by Mahjong Soul. (d) American (NMJL-card) mahjong is a small, fragmented, subscription-priced niche riding a large offline boom among US women. Chinese, Hong Kong and Taiwanese 4-player apps are a separate local "chess-and-card" (棋牌) category, close to social casino.

### Cited Findings
**(a) Mahjong solitaire / Shanghai-style pair matching**
- Sensor Tower files Vita Mahjong under the "Hypercasual Match Pair" sub-genre. Vita Mahjong's ad revenue went from $38M to $90M and made the largest absolute gain in that sub-genre, whose ad revenue rose by $105M [HARD] — [GameDev Reports summary of Sensor Tower Ad Monetization report, Jul 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- Match Pair, Sort and Match Swap titles each took about 7% of mobile game ad revenue in Feb–Apr 2026. Block was the largest puzzle sub-genre at 10%, and puzzle overall took 53% of mobile game ad revenue [HARD] — [mobilegamer.biz data digest](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-pokemon-champions-the-top-us-earners-funding-news-sensor-towers-big-ad-report-more/)
- AppMagic describes Vita Mahjong as "classic Mahjong Solitaire with larger tiles, cleaner visuals, and a slower pace", aimed at a broad casual audience, "especially older players" — [AppMagic, "Learnings from Learnings"](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/learnings-hypercasual-hits-and-megre/?hl=en)
- Other incumbents:
  - Mahjong Titan+ (Kristanix AS) is on Apple Arcade with no ads and no IAP. It has 4.8★ from 22K ratings, "over 3000 boards" and "a new Mahjong board a day" [HARD] — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1551538909)
  - Mahjong Epic is published by Kristanix Studios AS, free with IAP, on the Microsoft Store — [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9nblggh52253)
  - Netflix added a "Mahjong Solitaire" mobile exclusive in July 2022, with no ads and no IAP, included in the membership, and with a Stranger Things theme — [Game Developer, 26 Jul 2022](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/netflix-games-expands-with-two-new-mobile-exclusives)
  - Microsoft Mahjong was co-developed with Arkadium. It is ad-supported, with ads removable through a monthly or yearly Premium membership, and was ported to Android and iOS on 1 Feb 2021 as "Mahjong by Microsoft" (search-result summary of [Giant Bomb wiki](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Microsoft_Mahjong); the page itself returned 403 when fetched).

**(b) Triple-match / Tile Master / Tile Explorer-style**
- AppMagic, full-year 2025 against 2024 (data to 22 Oct 2025): the "Match-3: Tile" sub-genre's IAP revenue fell **30.87%**, from $115.1M to $79.6M. Over the same period Match-3 overall grew 4% ($4.4B→$4.6B), Block Puzzle grew 911% ($15.4M→$156M) and Fill & Organize grew 175% [HARD] — [GameDev Reports summary of AppMagic Monetization Report 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-games-monetization)
- AppMagic, H1 2025: "Match 3D" (3D triple-match) revenue was $193M (−12% YoY) on 49M installs (−34%). Match Factory! held a 56% share and Triple Match 3D 22%. **None of 97 new projects passed $100K/month** [HARD] — [GameDev Reports summary of AppMagic Casual H1'25](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-casual-games-in-h125)
- Triple Match 3D's "Mix & Match" offer ($2.99–$27.99) produced about 7.3% of revenue while it was live. Puzzle revenue in general comes mostly from LiveOps offers priced at $6–15 [HARD] — [GameDev Reports / AppMagic](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-games-monetization)
- Tile Explorer – Triple Match (Oakever Games, part of the same Learnings group as Vita Mahjong) added about $20M in Match Pair ad revenue in the Sensor Tower report period [HARD] — [GameDev Reports / Sensor Tower](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization). One App Store storefront (Saudi Arabia) showed 4.7★ from 4.7K ratings (search-result summary of [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/app/id6498883328)).

**(c1) Japanese Riichi (4-player, online PvP)**
- Mahjong Soul held a revenue share of more than 50% of Japanese mobile mahjong games between May 2022 and April 2024, and also ranked first by download share. Its revenue per download has been about $20 or more since around April 2021. Named competitors are SEGA's NET Mahjong MJ Mobile and KONAMI's Mahjong Fight Club Sp [HARD, Sensor Tower] — [Dengeki Online, Jun 2024](https://dengekionline.com/article/202406/7775)
- Mahjong Soul's lifetime revenue was nearly **$150M** from November 2019 to 17 May 2023. By country: Japan 90%, Taiwan 7%, Hong Kong 2%. Over May 2022–April 2023 its revenue was nearly double that of MJ Mobile (#2) [HARD, Sensor Tower] — [Sensor Tower JP blog, Jun 2023](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/mahjong-soul-code-geass-collaboration)
- Monetization is gacha (random character pulls) that is cosmetic only, with "not generous" rates — [COGconnected, Apr 2025](https://cogconnected.com/2025/04/mahjong-soul-how-an-anime-mahjong-game-climbed-into-steams-top-charts/)
- An editorial (not revenue-based) 2026 ranking of Japanese mahjong apps: 1. Mahjong Soul (雀魂), 2. Mahjong Fight Club Sp, 3. Mahjong Ichibangai — [smartlog.jp](https://smartlog.jp/177747)
- Riichi City is developed and published by Formirai Co., Ltd. on PC and mobile, with an update dated 2025-11-17 (search-result summary of [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/app/riichi-city/)). I found no download or revenue figures.

**(c2) Chinese / Hong Kong / Taiwanese 4-player**
- Tencent's 欢乐麻将 (Happy Mahjong) is described as a free 3D mahjong game from Tencent IEG that bundles many regional rule sets (e.g., 血流成河 and 武汉麻将). The Xiaomi store page shows about 30.57M downloads; the date and counting basis are unclear [EST] — [Xiaomi Game Center](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1543958016_1721729140383_149)
- Taiwan's popular 16-tile apps include 神來也, 宅神爺, 明星3缺1 and 愛台灣打麻將 (an editorial popularity ranking). 神來也 and 宅神爺 bundle poker and other games, and 明星3缺1 adds slot machines and poker, i.e., social-casino style — [mybest Taiwan](https://tw.my-best.com/115456)

**(c3) American Mahjong (NMJL card)**
- This style uses a standardized annual card of preset hands and eight jokers; in LA the dominant styles are Hong Kong, American and Taiwanese — [NBC Los Angeles, 5 Aug 2026](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/national-international/why-mahjong-rising-popularity-los-angeles/3923210/)
- The National Mah Jongg League (NMJL) issues a new card every spring, and "any app that plays American Mahjong needs to update to it". Several apps require the user to own the physical card — [Bam Good Time roundup, Oct 2026 (written by a competitor)](https://bamgoodtime.com/blog/best-american-mahjong-apps-2026)
- Apps are priced as subscriptions clustered at $5–7/month or $50–60/year (see Q2 table) [HARD, store listings].

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The economics split cleanly. Solitaire is ad-first (IAA) with "remove ads" and subscriptions as a secondary layer. Triple-match is IAP/LiveOps-first. Riichi is gacha/IAP with very high revenue per download (about $20+). American Mahjong is subscription SaaS-like with tiny install bases. Taiwan and China are social casino / virtual currency.
- [INFERENCE] Sensor Tower and AppMagic do not have a "mahjong" category. Solitaire mahjong is counted as "Match Pair" (ad revenue) or sits under puzzle/board, while Riichi and Chinese apps are counted under board/card. Any "mahjong market size" figure must be assembled title by title.

### Gaps
- I found no source sizing "mahjong" as a single category (downloads or revenue) for 2025–2026.
- I found no 2025–2026 revenue figures for Tencent Happy Mahjong, Taiwanese 16-tile apps, Sega MJ, Konami Mahjong Fight Club Sp, Riichi City or Tenhou.

## 3. Case study: Vita Mahjong (Vita Studio / Learnings / Oakever) and other breakouts

### Takeaway
Vita Mahjong is a classic pair-matching mahjong solitaire game repositioned explicitly "for seniors": large tiles, simple controls, offline play, no timers or lives, and a "relax/brain health" framing. A hypercasual-native Chinese publisher (Learnings, founded 2016 by an ex-Google AdMob China head) scaled it with aggressive Meta UA and creative volume. It grew from launch in about late 2023 / early 2024 to more than 237M installs, #5 worldwide downloads (Feb 2026) and about $90M of ad revenue in five months of 2026. The publisher clones winners across adjacent casual niches (a "Vita ... for Seniors" brand family), which has brought lawsuits and platform disputes.

### Cited Findings
**Corporate background**
- Oakever Games also trades as Learnings and publishes Vita Mahjong under the name Vita Studio. The company was founded as Lexin Shengwen in February 2016 by Liu Yiwei and Li Zhong; Liu ran AdMob at Google China. It has 200–300 staff, 85% of them in R&D — [mobilegamer.biz, 21 Sep 2026](https://mobilegamer.biz/vita-mahjong-amaze-go-and-meowdoku-maker-oakever-games-fights-cloning-accusations/)
- The App Store seller is "VITA STUDIO PTE. LTD." (a Singapore entity) [HARD] — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vita-mahjong/id6468921495). FoxData also calls it a "Singapore developer" — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/jp/blogs/how-vita-mahjong-stayed-1-in-the-us-app-store-and-what-it-means-for-app-growth-in-2025/)
- Learnings is a Chinese hypercasual publisher founded in 2016 with more than 1.7B total downloads, which applies hypercasual UA know-how to larger genres. Its ads "lean on misleading ads with varied core themes and art styles" — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/learnings-hypercasual-hits-and-megre/?hl=en)

  - Third-party estimates of about 400K daily downloads and **$150K+/day ad revenue** [EST]; 100M+ Google Play downloads.

- Hungry Studio (Block Blast) sued over Oakever's QBlock, and a judge ruled for Hungry Studio on 15 Apr 2026. Avia Games filed in Dec 2025, alleging Oakever pasted its own logos over 8 Avia ads. Arrows Go was renamed Amaze Go. Apple confirmed that developers used its content-dispute process against Oakever apps — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/vita-mahjong-amaze-go-and-meowdoku-maker-oakever-games-fights-cloning-accusations/)

**Other notable breakouts and adjacent hits, 2023–2026**
- Block Blast! (Hungry Studio): #1 game by downloads, Jan 2025–Jul 2026, with 526.0M; $127M ad revenue Jan–May 2026 — [Singular](https://www.singular.net/blog/top-mobile-games/); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-game-ad-monetization)
- Hybrid-casual puzzles in H1 2025: Block revenue x12, Screw x2.8, Sort x2.2 YoY; more than 2,500 projects launched — [GameDev Reports / AppMagic](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-casual-games-in-h125)
- Mahjong Soul's collaborations act as revenue spikes:
  - Code Geass (Apr–May 2023): about $1.6M on day 1 and more than $11M over the event — [Sensor Tower JP](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/mahjong-soul-code-geass-collaboration)
  - Kaguya-sama (Golden Week 2022): more than $12M — [Sensor Tower JP](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/mahjong-soul-code-geass-collaboration)
  - Blue Archive (Apr 2024): record DAU and MAU — [Dengeki](https://dengekionline.com/article/202406/7775)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Vita Mahjong's success formula:
  1. An evergreen, public-domain mechanic with a high-intent search term ("mahjong").
  2. Explicit age targeting in the store title and listing ("for Seniors"), which gives an ASO and positioning edge plus trust.
  3. A retention-first onboarding with ads delayed until session 2, which makes the $5.99 ad removal feel valuable.
  4. Heavy interstitial density afterwards, monetizing a high-eCPM, ad-tolerant, Tier-1-heavy audience (US/UK).
  5. Brute-force Meta creative testing with "brain training/IQ" hooks.
  6. Fast, weekly LiveOps cadence and 62 languages.
  7. Brand-family cross-promotion (about 12 "Vita ... for Seniors" apps).
- [INFERENCE] An indie team cannot replicate Vita's UA scale. Its ~$215M/yr ad run rate implies very large daily UA budgets. Competing head-on in generic "mahjong solitaire for seniors" on paid UA is the least attractive path.
- [INFERENCE] The D1 46% / D7 26% retention estimate is high for a casual ad-funded game and supports the idea that senior mahjong players are unusually sticky.
- [INFERENCE] FoxData's ">1M downloads in the past 30 days" (Sep 2025) conflicts with AppMagic's 9.3M/month (Jun 2026) and Sensor Tower's ~11.5M/month average over Jan 2025–Jul 2026. The FoxData figure is probably iOS-only or a single country.

## 4. Audience demographics and play patterns

### Takeaway
The evidence that solitaire mahjong skews older and female is strong in positioning and circumstantial in data. The leader brands itself "for Seniors"; AARP data shows US 50+ gamers are mobile-first and puzzle-heavy; CrazyGames data says US mahjong players skew to women over 55. Japanese Riichi skews younger, anime-oriented and (per Sensor Tower) relatively young and female for the genre. American Mahjong's 2024–2026 boom centers on US women aged about 30–40 ("Momjong"), with Gen Z, sororities and older Jewish women's networks as additional pillars.

## 5. Market trends

### Takeaway
Puzzle is the growth engine of casual mobile, with both IAP (~$8.8–10B in 2025) and ad revenue (53% of game ad revenue) rising. But growth is concentrated in new hybrid-casual mechanics (block, sort, arrow) and in ad-first "older casual" hits like Vita Mahjong. Triple-match / tile IAP is in clear decline. American Mahjong is a fast-growing offline cultural trend whose digital side is still fragmented and young. Japanese Riichi is mature and dominated by Mahjong Soul's collaboration-driven LiveOps.

### Cited Findings
**Puzzle growth**
- Puzzle IAP grew from $7.7B to $8.8B (+14.7%) in 2025 per AppMagic — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-games-monetization). Sensor Tower puts puzzle IAP above $10B (+14%) per a search-result summary of Naavik — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/what-leading-match-3-and-merge-games-do-differently/). Puzzle reached $8.1B in H1 2026 after nearly 20% growth (Sensor Tower H1 2026 index) — [Singular](https://www.singular.net/blog/top-mobile-games/)

## 6. Gaps and opportunities a newcomer could exploit

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced opening for a small team is **American Mahjong (NMJL-style)**: demand is booming offline, the digital field is fragmented, incumbents are small, users already pay $50–70/yr, and the official NMJL app has negligible traction. Secondary openings are learn-to-play and social features (playing with friends and family, club tools) and niche or localized solitaire variants distributed via web. A head-on generic "mahjong solitaire for seniors" built on paid UA, or a triple-match game, is the least attractive option given Vita's scale and the sub-genre's decline.

### Cited Findings
- Incumbent American Mahjong apps and their scale (US App Store): Mahjong 4 Friends has 26K ratings, The Mahjong App 7.9K, Real Mah Jongg 5.7K and NMJL Online 4 (1.8★) [HARD] — [Mahjong 4 Friends](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-4-friends-multiplayer/id1552704332); [The Mahjong App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-mahjong-app/id6763795120); [Real Mah Jongg](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/real-mah-jongg/id1249840399); [NMJL Online](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nmjl-online/id6476651173)
- The card dependency is a structural constraint. NMJL Online requires buying the physical card plus a membership. Real Mah Jongg and Mahj Parlour require the user to own the card. Apps must update to each spring's new card. Some apps also support alternative cards: Marvelous (Mahjong 4 Friends), the American Mahjong Society card (The Mahjong App), and The Big Card and Mah Jongg Press (Real Mah Jongg, MahJongg4Fun) — [Bam Good Time](https://bamgoodtime.com/blog/best-american-mahjong-apps-2026); [NMJL Online listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nmjl-online/id6476651173); [The Mahjong App listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-mahjong-app/id6763795120)
- Proven price points: $4.99–$6.99/month or $49.99–$60/year; lifetime $149.99 (The Mahjong App); one-time $17.99 (Eight Bam 2026 hands) — store listings above and [Bam Good Time](https://bamgoodtime.com/blog/best-american-mahjong-apps-2026)
- Unmet "social/club" layer: new club-management apps charge organizers $1.99–$49/month (Bam Good Time Club $19/$49, TileTime $1.99/$14.99, Mahjy $0.99–$19.99 plus a 2% seat fee), and Let's Mahj claims 2,400+ players — [Bam Good Time](https://bamgoodtime.com/blog/best-american-mahjong-apps-2026)
- Mahjong 4 Friends already bundles American, Chinese, Hong Kong, British and HK Old Style rules with ad and "Pro" IAPs, which shows a multi-variant social model works at small scale — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahjong-4-friends-multiplayer/id1552704332)
- Riichi outside Asia is small: about 3% of Mahjong Soul's audience is English-speaking, and its English Steam version averages about 400–550 concurrent players — [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/2025/04/mahjong-soul-how-an-anime-mahjong-game-climbed-into-steams-top-charts/); [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/mahjong-soul)
- In Japan, Mahjong Soul holds more than 50% of mahjong revenue, with ~$20+ revenue per download and IP collaborations — [Dengeki](https://dengekionline.com/article/202406/7775)
- Triple-match faces high failure odds: 0 of 97 Match 3D launches above $100K/mo in H1 2025, and Match-3 Tile IAP fell 31% in 2025 — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-casual-games-in-h125); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-games-monetization)
- Clone risk from big publishers: Learnings / Oakever has faced lawsuits and Apple content disputes for cloning (Block Blast, Avia ads, Arrows) — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/vita-mahjong-amaze-go-and-meowdoku-maker-oakever-games-fights-cloning-accusations/)
- India is the #2 web market for mahjong clicks (13.5%), and the UK the #3 (6.7%) — [IPS News](https://ipsnews.net/business/2025/09/09/surge-in-mahjong-popularity-a-country-level-breakdown/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] **Opportunity A: American Mahjong "learn + play with your group" app.** This audience pays subscriptions rather than relying on UA-funded ads.
  - Differentiators: polished onboarding for the ~30–45-year-old newcomers (interactive lessons, hand-finder and "which hands am I close to" coaching, Charleston tutor); private-table play for real-life groups with video and voice; club, league and tournament tools; and a beautiful, design-forward "lifestyle" aesthetic that mirrors the $350–475 designer-set trend.
  - Key risk: NMJL card IP. The League sells the card and runs its own app. Requiring "bring your own card" (as Real Mah Jongg and Mahj Parlour do), licensing, or supporting alternative cards (Marvelous, AMS, Mah Jongg Press) are the observed workarounds. This needs legal review.
- [INFERENCE] **Opportunity B: accessible / localized mahjong solitaire niches**, e.g., large-print plus high-contrast plus colorblind modes, VoiceOver support, culturally themed tile sets, and underserved languages or markets (Brazil and India appear in Vita's UA mix). It should be distributed through web portals (CrazyGames, where the US is the #1 audience) and organic ASO rather than heavy paid UA. Revenue ceiling is modest unless UA scales.
- [INFERENCE] **Opportunity C: social and family solitaire** (async co-op boards, "play with grandkids", shared daily puzzles). The leader is strictly single-player, so this is a gap in its feature set. I found no evidence measuring demand for it, so it is speculative.
- [INFERENCE] **Opportunity D: Western learn-to-play Riichi.** Product gap is real (the English audience is underserved), but market evidence suggests a small, enthusiast-sized revenue pool. Better as a secondary mode or a later expansion than as a core business.
- [INFERENCE] **Avoid:** generic triple-match, and a head-to-head generic senior solitaire game funded by paid UA against Vita Studio, whose ~$215M/yr ad run rate (inferred) funds UA a small team cannot match.

### Gaps
- No data on NMJL licensing terms or enforcement against third-party apps, and no NMJL statement on digital rights. A legal question for the plan.
- No quantitative demand data (downloads or revenue) for American Mahjong apps from Sensor Tower or AppMagic, so market size is inferred from ratings counts and offline signals.
- No evidence measuring demand for social or family solitaire features.
- No CPI or eCPM benchmarks specific to mahjong or senior audiences were found within the search budget (likely covered by the monetization/UA research stream).
